About Ember & Bean

Our Mission

To make exceptional, ethically sourced coffee simple and approachable. We pay growers well above fair-trade prices, roast in small batches for peak flavor, and share everything we know about brewing so every customer can make a great cup at home.

Our Story

Ember & Bean started in 2019 with a secondhand 5-kilo roaster in a garage in Charlotte, North Carolina. What began as weekend batches for friends and neighbors grew into a farmers-market stall, and then into the roastery and café we run today.

We still roast every batch by hand, still taste every roast before it ships, and still know most of our regulars by name. The name comes from the glow of the roaster's drum — that moment when the beans crack and the whole room smells like caramel.
