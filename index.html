Welcome to Ember & Bean

Ember & Bean is an independent roastery that sources specialty coffee directly from growers in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Sumatra. Alongside our beans, we carry the brewing gear and drinkware we use every day behind our own counter, so you can brew café-quality coffee at home.
